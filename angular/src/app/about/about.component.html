Our Story

Cria Tu Handmade started as one person, a needle, and a lot of felt. Ten years and 644 sales later, it's still just that — every ornament, every personalised name, every stitch, made by hand right here in Witney, England.

"Cria Tu" means create yours — because the best gifts are the ones made just for you.

— Cria Tu

What We Make

Felt decorations and ornaments, hand-embroidered and hand-stitched — Christmas tree decorations, Easter eggs, felt bees and ladybugs, personalised gifts and more. Every piece can be customised with a name, a letter, or a message, just for the person you're making it for.

Why Handmade Matters

No mass production — just one pair of hands, careful stitching, and a lot of care for detail.
